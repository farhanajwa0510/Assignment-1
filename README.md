# Project Title: Web-Based Detection of Harmful Elsagate Content in Children's Entertainment Videos Using Deep Learning 

This project helps parents and guardians monitor their children's YouTube watchlist by screening videos for potential Elsagate-related content without relying solely on the platform's automated content moderation and filtering that may overlook these aspects.

## Problem statement
Children who are in the developing stage mentally and emotionally as well as their parents or guardians are affected by the presence of inappropriate content on YouTube. This is due to harmful content like Elsagate, which depicts popular characters in inappropriate acts and can slip through already existing content moderation and filtering systems. Since children from the age of 12 and below lack critical thinking skills to recognise potentially harmful content, parents or guardians may find it difficult to monitor their child's YouTube's watchlist. Long-term exposure to disturbing content may negatively affect the child's well-being mentally.
