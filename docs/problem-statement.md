## Why does it matter?
As a parent or a guardian, it is important to monitor your child's YouTube's watchlist to protect them from being exposed to harmful content. Children from the age of 12 and below may lack necessary critical thinking skills that have yet to develop, hence actively monitoring the videos they watch can contribute in quick interventions in case they have been exposed. Long-term exposure to disturbing YouTube videos that contain content like Elsagate may cause symptoms of loss of appetite, insomnia, crying fits and tension among children.
