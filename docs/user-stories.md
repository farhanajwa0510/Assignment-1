# User Stories

Format: As a [type of user], I want [goal], so that [reason or benefit].

1. As a parent or guardian, I want to retrieve up to 10 available videos from my selected YouTube channel, so that I could identify potentially concerning Elsafate content for further review. (FR1)

Acceptance criteria (1) 
Acceptance criteria 
a. I can enter and search for the YouTube channel I would like to review.
b. I can view up to 10 available videos should be retrieved after YouTube channel is selected.

2. As a parent or guardian, I want to view the flagged video frames and the concern level (low, medium, high) of each videos, so I can identify which videos that need closer review. (FR2)

Acceptance criteria (2): 
a. I can enter and search for the YouTube channel I would like to review.
b. I can view up to 10 available videos should be retrieved after YouTube channel is selected. 

3. As a parent or guardian, I want to view my past YouTube channel screening history that was done in the last 30 days, so that I can refer to the channels I have reviewed. (FR3)

Acceptance criteria (3) 
a. There should be a history tab in the dashboard that is accessible for me to use.
b. At least one screening activity must have been done in the past 30 days for its results to appear in the history tab, otherwise there would be no records displayed.
