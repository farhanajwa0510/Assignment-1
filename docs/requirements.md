# Requirements

## Functional requirements (what the system must DO)
1. The system shall able to retrieve up to 10 videos of the user's selected YouTube Channel to run the screening process (FR1) 

2. The system shall display the screening results that include flagged video frames with its timestamps and an overall concern level (low, medium, high) (FR2)

3. The system shall display the history of past reviewed YouTube channels and its screened videos selected by the user within the past month or 30 days (FR3)

4. The system shall allow parents and guardians to register for an account (FR4)

5. The system shall allow registered users to log into their accounts using their respective credentials (FR5)

## Non-functional requirements (how the system must PERFORM)
1. The system shall take 5 seconds to retrieve up to 10 available videos after user selects the YouTube channel (NFR1)

1. The system shall display the screening results of a video within 10 seconds after analysis is done (NFR2)

3. The system shall users to access the main's dashboards functions within 3 seconds (NFR3)
